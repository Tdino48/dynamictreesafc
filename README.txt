This mod adds in the 45 new trees from AFC to dynamic trees. All of them have unique growth habits, and I
also adjusted some requirements in the rainfall min-max ranges to make them more realistic and add diversity.

I added my own spin on the Flame Of The Forest and Red Silk Cotton trees, making their leaves orange and red
year round to add some nice diversity in the forests. Also, the Coast Redwood's size was obviously
inspired by the real-life Coastal Redwood tree, so I made the logs only be obtained by chopping down
the tree with bronze+ axe tiers. The sticks and the seeds still drop if cut with copper or stone,
just no logs. I thought it would be too OP to allow players to get that number of logs from the
tree with little game progress.
